GoalCheck is a local-first, open-source planner that stays true to the original idea: goals → categories → tasks, progress rings, priorities, notes, subtasks, dependency locks and a drift-free Pomodoro timer. No account, no sign-up, no analytics, no trackers — and zero network access for core features. The Android build is shipped with the INTERNET permission REMOVED: your data physically cannot leave your device.

• Boards with categories, tasks, notes, subtasks and dependency locks
• Recurring daily/weekly categories, quick-paste rapid add, search + filters
• Pomodoro focus timer with floating pill — works offline forever
• Encrypted backups: AES-256-GCM ".gcenc" vault exports (passphrase + PBKDF2)
• Report card: shareable canvas summary (week / month / all)
• Community packs: import curated goal packs from URL or pasted JSON
• Capture text from anywhere: Android share sheet, Quick Settings tile, PWA share target
• Android home-screen widget + Quick Settings tile
• High-contrast accessibility theme + text size 90–130%
• Light & dark themes, completion confetti, reduce-effects battery saver
• English, हिन्दी, Español and Deutsch built in
• Full JSON export/import — your data is never locked in
• MIT-licensed FOSS; fonts bundled locally; no proprietary dependencies

Want more? GoalCheck Advance adds Kanban/Matrix/Mind-map/Analytics views, a week planner, habits & achievements, RPG levels, 12 themes with particle atmospheres and an audio studio — as a separate app. Same storage schema: upgrading keeps every byte.

No ads. No tracking. No cloud. Just your goals, on your device.
