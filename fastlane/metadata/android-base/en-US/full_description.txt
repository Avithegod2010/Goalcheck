GoalCheck is a local-first, open-source planner that stays true to the original idea: goals → categories → tasks, progress rings, priorities, notes, subtasks, dependency locks and a drift-free Pomodoro timer. No account, no analytics, no network. The Android app is built with the INTERNET permission REMOVED — your data physically cannot leave your device.

• Boards with categories, tasks, notes, subtasks and dependency locks
• Recurring daily/weekly categories, quick-paste rapid add, search + filters
• Pomodoro focus timer with floating pill, works offline forever
• Light & dark themes, completion confetti, reduce-effects battery saver
• Full JSON export/import — your data is never locked in
• MIT-licensed open source
• Want more? GoalCheck Advance adds Kanban/Matrix/Mind-map/Analytics views, habits, achievements, RPG levels, 11 themes with particle atmospheres and an audio studio — switchable from inside this app if you install the Advance edition, or via the separate Advance app. Same storage schema: upgrading keeps every byte.
