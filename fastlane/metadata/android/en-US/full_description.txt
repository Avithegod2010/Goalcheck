GoalCheck Advance is the complete edition of GoalCheck — a local-first, open-source productivity workspace. Your data stays 100% on your device: no account, no analytics, no network. The Android app is built with the INTERNET permission REMOVED, so "local-only" is enforced by the operating system itself.

PLAN
• Boards → categories → tasks with notes, subtasks and dependency locks
• Kanban, Eisenhower Matrix, Mind-map and Analytics views
• Habit tracker with streak flames, 13 achievements, due dates with Overdue/Today/This-week badges, recurring daily/weekly tasks
• Rapid add + quick-paste parser for entire checklists

FOCUS
• Drift-free Pomodoro timer that survives reloads and runs in the background
• Floating focus pill, Zen mode, local notifications when the app is backgrounded

GROW
• RPG gamification: XP, levels, combos, skills and auras
• 365-day activity heatmap and per-goal focus-time analytics
• Switch to the minimal GoalCheck (Normal) edition anytime from Settings — same storage, zero data loss

MAKE IT YOURS
• 11 themes with 13 particle engines, cursor physics, atmosphere presets
• Audio studio with 7 generative ambient streams and 4 completion chime packs
• Theme JSON sharing, goal templates, command palette (Ctrl+K)

PRIVACY & FREEDOM
• MIT-licensed open source — audit every line
• Full JSON export/import: your data is never locked in
• Optional sync-file: auto-save your workspace JSON into a Syncthing/WebDAV folder you control
