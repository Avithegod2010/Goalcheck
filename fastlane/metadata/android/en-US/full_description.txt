GoalCheck Advance is the complete edition of GoalCheck — a local-first, open-source productivity workspace. Everything runs on your device: no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no trackers, and zero network access for core features. The Android build goes one step further: the INTERNET permission is REMOVED, so "local-only" is enforced by the operating system itself, not just a privacy policy.

PLAN
• Boards → categories → tasks with notes, subtasks, priorities and dependency locks
• Views: checklist grid, Kanban, Eisenhower Matrix, Mind-map and Analytics
• Week view — a 7-day planner over the due-date/recurrence engine
• Habit tracker with streaks and streak freezes; 13 achievements; seasonal challenges
• Due dates with Overdue/Today/This-week badges; recurring daily/weekly tasks
• Rapid add + quick-paste parser; share text into the app from anywhere (Android share sheet, Quick Settings tile, PWA share target)

FOCUS
• Drift-free Pomodoro timer — timestamp-based, survives reloads and backgrounding
• Floating focus pill, Zen mode, local notifications when a session ends in the background
• Per-goal focus-time analytics, attributed automatically from Pomodoro sessions

GROW
• RPG layer: XP, levels, combos, skills and auras + 365-day activity heatmap
• Report card: shareable canvas summary of your week, month or all-time

MAKE IT YOURS
• 12 themes including a high-contrast accessibility theme, text size 90–130%
• 13 particle engines, cursor physics, atmosphere presets (all rendered locally)
• Audio studio: 7 generative ambient streams + 4 chime packs, synthesized on-device
• Command palette (Ctrl+K), goal templates, theme JSON sharing, community packs
• English, हिन्दी, Español and Deutsch built in

PRIVACY & FREEDOM
• Local-first: data stays in on-device storage, schema-versioned with migrations
• Data Doctor: integrity scan, storage meter and safe auto-repairs
• Encrypted backups: AES-256-GCM ".gcenc" vault exports (passphrase + PBKDF2)
• Optional WebDAV vault sync and sync-file auto-save — you pick the server (e.g. a Syncthing/WebDAV folder you control)
• Full JSON export/import — your data is never locked in
• MIT-licensed FOSS: audit every line; fonts bundled locally; no proprietary dependencies
• Two editions, one data format: GoalCheck (base, free) and GoalCheck Advance share the same storage schema — switch from Settings or install the other app without losing a byte
• Runs as an installable PWA in any browser and as a zero-permission Android app

No ads. No tracking. No cloud. Just your goals, on your device.
